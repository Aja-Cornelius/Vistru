System Overview

One platform.
Every stakeholder.

A single connected system that keeps every party accountable at every stage of your construction project.

🏗️

Client & Engineer Flow

The client uploads land documents and drawings. Engineers receive notifications, review drawings, and submit BOQs. The client selects the best BOQ and the lawyer prepares a binding contract. Payment flows through escrow, released per completed milestone.

🏪

Supplier Integration

Suppliers display materials and store location. Clients browse nearby suppliers, select materials per the BOQ, and pay into escrow. Once delivery is confirmed on site, escrow releases payment to the supplier automatically.

⚖️

Legal & Arbitration

Platform lawyers are onboarded by Vistru. They verify land titles, draft contracts, and handle arbitration. If a dispute arises, either client or engineer can file through the platform. Location-based assignment ensures proximity.

📹

CCTV + AI Monitoring

Vistru agents physically deploy CCTV cameras to sites. The AI interprets footage — tracking milestones, counting materials, reporting progress. Clients receive live feeds and AI reports enabling confident payment decisions from anywhere.

🔒

Escrow System

All client payments are received and held by escrow. A percentage charge applies to every transaction. Funds are only released when the client gives explicit confirmation — protecting all parties at every stage.

💼

Engineer Project Loans

Because engineer payment depends on milestone completion, engineers can apply for project loans to manage workers' wages and site costs. Escrow automatically settles any outstanding loan when milestone payment is released.
